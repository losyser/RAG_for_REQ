[package] Paper v4 - Reliability Notes Detail [Cooling Reliability - Selected Notes]
«requirement»
Tropical Cooling Capacity
id = "ER-R001-04"
The system shall maintain stable cooling 
capacity in high-temperature tropical waters. 
Specific cooling capacity parameters shall be 
confirmed against the main engine 
manufacturer's Technical Data.
«requirement»
Load-Change Temperature Control
id = "ER-R001-03"
The main engine cooling water system shall be 
equipped with automatic temperature regulation 
that adjusts the cooling water temperature as 
the main engine load changes and maintains it 
within the limits specified by the main engine 
manufacturer. The system shall provide high-
temperature alarms and automatic protection, 
and shall issue an alarm when the cooling 
water temperature exceeds the high-alarm 
setpoint.
«requirement»
Tropical Cooling Design Basis
id = "ER-R001-02"
The main engine cooling water system shall 
maintain stable cooling capacity in high-
temperature tropical waters. The maximum 
seawater temperature used for system design 
shall be confirmed against the project Design 
Basis, and the cooling water temperature shall 
remain within the limits specified by the main 
engine manufacturer.
